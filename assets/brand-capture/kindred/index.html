// How it works

Three steps. That's the whole thing.

No listing fees, no feed algorithm, no pressure. Just post, chat, drop off or pick up.

STEP 01
📸

Post a give or an ask.

Snap a photo, tag it GIVE or NEED, and Kindred drops it on the map for neighbours within walking distance. You can even note "open to swap for…" if you're keen.

STEP 02
💬

A neighbour messages.

Someone nearby pings you in-app. Sort out a time, a pickup spot, or a community meetup point. All the chat happens inside Kindred.

STEP 03
🤝

Drop it off or pick it up.

Leave it on the porch for pickup, meet at a shared spot, or have a yarn while you hand it across — whatever suits you both. Mark it complete and you both earn Kindness Points.

Back the mission, get a little extra.

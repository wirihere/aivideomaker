# Render Suggestions — Cross-Render Patterns

This is the improvement library. It distils recurring findings from `LEDGER.md`
into reusable rules of thumb. Append a section when a pattern shows up in two or
more renders. Keep entries short, sized (e.g. "+12% font" not "bigger font"), and
actionable (point at a template or token).

Structure:
- One H2 per pattern.
- Sub-bullets with the evidence (which renders surfaced it).
- A "next time" line that future runs can apply.

## Watch for template seed-copy leakage

- _Initial entry._ Templates ship with placeholder strings ("HEADLINE", "BENEFIT",
  "Q1 / Q2 / Q3", etc.). When `applyCopyToTemplate()` doesn't cover an id, the
  seed string survives into the render and the audio narrates a different scene
  than the visuals show.
- Next time: the verifier flags `placeholder leakage` whenever visible text
  matches a known seed-copy string list — see `scripts/verify-render.mjs`'s
  `SEED_COPY_PATTERNS`. Add new patterns there as new templates ship.

## Music tempo and scene timing

Empirical rules of thumb derived from analysing every track in
`assets/music/` (see `docs/music-template-alignment.md` and
`assets/music/alignment.json`, regenerated by
`scripts/analyze-music-alignment.mjs`).

- **Curator BPM beats estimator BPM.** Onset-autocorrelation locks to half
  tempo on tracks with strong bar-level chord motion (e.g. `kinetic-pop-top`
  reads 66.7 BPM but is curated 120 BPM and is correct at 120). Trust the
  hand-tagged BPM in `assets/music-shortlists/<vibe>.json` when present;
  fall back to the estimator only for un-curated tracks.
- **Kinetic templates with ≤4 cuts: pick the densest drop track.**
  `kinetic-pop-top.mp3` has ~30 RMS drops over 90 s (drop density 0.33/s)
  vs `kinetic-01-motivational.mp3` at 0.06/s. The dense track scores higher
  on `before-after-20s` (5 cuts in 20 s) because more drops fall within
  ±0.6 s of a cut. For longer kinetic comps with sparse cuts, the calmer
  track is fine.
- **Documentary/warm templates: prefer swells, penalise drops, ignore RMS
  std.** Swells (slow monotonic builds, ≥25% rise over 5 s) are a virtue
  for narrative beds — `documentary-top.mp3` has 42 swells in 226 s and
  scores 99 on `case-study-60s`. Sharp drops (>30% jump between 1 s
  windows) are noise: at >0.12 drops/s the track is flagged "may fight
  narration".
- **Pixabay loudness-normalised tracks all sit in std=0.04..0.09.** Energy
  std-dev alone can't separate kinetic from quiet — kicks and pads both
  normalise to similar RMS. Use BPM bin + drop count as the kinetic
  signal, not RMS variability.
- **Cut-aligned drops add ~3 score points each (capped at 18).** A track
  with 3 of its drops landing within 0.6 s of a scene cut beats a track
  with 0. Worth wiring into the picker when ranking similarly-scored
  candidates.
- **Same-vibe curator match is +12, cross-vibe is −4.** The curator's
  hand-grouping carries more signal than the auto-features when both
  agree, and it's a useful tie-breaker.
- **Watch for byte-identical duplicates.** `documentary-01-strings.mp3` ==
  `documentary-top.mp3` and `energetic-inspiring-pop.mp3` ==
  `kinetic-pop-top.mp3` (same MD5). Either prune one of each pair or
  treat them as aliases; otherwise the picker can return effectively the
  same track twice in different slots.
- Next time: when a render's verifier flags music-mismatch, regenerate
  `alignment.json` and look at the per-template ranked list — a track that
  scores below 60 on its assigned template is the most likely culprit.

## How tracks were scored

Each track is decoded to mono 8 kHz PCM, then we extract:

- **Duration** (samples / SR)
- **RMS envelope** in 1 s windows (mean, std, min, max)
- **Drops** (>30% RMS jump between adjacent windows)
- **Swells** (monotonic >25% rise across a 5 s window)
- **BPM** via onset-autocorrelation in the 50–180 BPM range

Scoring favours BPM-in-range (+30), energy std inside the vibe's comfort
band (+20), drops/swells landing within 0.6 s of a scene cut (kinetic only),
duration covering the comp (+20), and curator-vibe match (+12).

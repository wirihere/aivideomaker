### "There are three doors. Only one of them opens."

- **Render:** methodology-45s for Oraculum Institutum (2026-04-28)
- **Slot:** b0-promise (cold open, B0)
- **Technique(s):** Caples question hook + Halbert specificity + Sugarman slippery slide
- **Why it worked:**
  - "Three" is concrete (Halbert: specificity beats abstraction). The brand could have said "many doors" or "several paths" — losing all the hook's power.
  - "Only one of them opens" creates an unspoken question — *which one?* — that the listener has to play the rest of the video to answer (Sugarman: line 1's only job is to make you read line 2).
  - Caples question hook in disguise: the line is a statement, but it sets up an interrogative that the audience completes silently in their head.
- **Reusable for:** any contemplative template opening with a methodology / choice / threshold metaphor. Adaptable as: "There are three [thresholds]. Only one of them [verb]."
- **NOT reusable for:** kinetic-pop (wrong tone — too still), warm-community (wrong distance — too oracular), documentary (wrong stance — documentary is broadcast, this is intimate).
- **Sentence stats:** 9 words / 2 sentences / lone-short final clause for rhythm.

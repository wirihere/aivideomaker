### "Three steps. One truth at a time."

- **Render:** methodology-45s for Oraculum Institutum (2026-04-28, v1)
- **Slot:** b4-outcome (closing line)
- **Why cut:** Author-invented during template scaffolding — violates Part 7 rule S14 (no fabricated content lines). The brand never wrote this anywhere; it was Claude's filler that read like a fortune-cookie aphorism.
- **What replaced it:** "Just truth." (canon from singularity-convergence reference build)
- **Anti-pattern to avoid:** outcome lines that sound like they belong on a coffee mug. If the line could be sold on Etsy as wall art, the brand probably didn't write it. Generic distilled wisdom is the opposite of brand voice.
- **Tagged technique violation:** Hopkins (every claim needs proof — this line claimed nothing real, so it had nothing to prove) + Ogilvy (facts not adjectives — "one truth at a time" is empty bombast).

## Anti-patterns surfaced from this rejection

When writing closing/outcome lines, refuse to ship if:

1. The line could appear on a generic motivational poster
2. A competitor in the same category could say the exact same line word-for-word
3. The line uses abstract distilled wisdom without naming the brand's actual claim
4. Claude wrote it because the slot needed filling, not because the brand earned it

When in doubt, leave the slot empty (visual-only with hairline + emblem) or pull a phrase verbatim from the brand's H1.

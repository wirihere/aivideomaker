ConsentMate is a clerical drafting service, not a law firm, planning consultancy, or engineering practice. You stay the applicant. You stay in control.

What ConsentMate does

ConsentMate provides a clerical drafting service for resource consent applications under the Resource Management Act 1991. We prepare draft documents — including Assessments of Environmental Effects, cover letters, and application forms — from information you supply.

What ConsentMate does not do

  • We do not provide legal advice, planning advice, engineering advice, or design advice.
  • We are not lawyers, licensed planners, licensed building practitioners, or engineers.
  • We do not guarantee that your resource consent will be granted.
  • We do not submit applications on your behalf unless you have purchased the Done-For-You package.

Your responsibilities

You are the applicant. You are responsible for:

  • The accuracy of all information you provide to us.
  • Reviewing all draft documents before submission to council.
  • Obtaining any professional reports required (e.g. geotechnical, surveyor, engineering).
  • Paying all council fees directly to council.
  • Complying with all council requirements and conditions of consent.

Pricing & payment

Our fees are as stated on our website at the time you submit your application. Council fees are separate and not included. Payment is due before delivery of your draft documents. During our first few months, services may be offered at reduced or no cost.

Turnaround

We aim to deliver draft documents within 2–3 business days of receiving a completed intake form. This is an estimate, not a guarantee. Complex applications may take longer.

Limitation of liability

Our total liability to you is limited to the fee you paid for the service. We are not liable for any loss arising from council decisions, delays, or requests for further information. We are not liable for any errors in information you provided to us.

Intellectual property

Documents we prepare for you are yours to use for the purpose of your resource consent application. Our templates, processes, and systems remain our intellectual property.

Changes to these terms

We may update these terms from time to time. The version on our website at the time you submit your application applies to your engagement.

Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of New Zealand. Any disputes will be resolved in the courts of New Zealand.

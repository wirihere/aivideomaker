E589 · Encyclopedia Article ★ · image
Drift Atelier
From The Free Encyclopedia

Drift Atelier (also known simply as Drift) is a small Japanese design studio founded in 2010 by Solen Park, based in Shibuya, Tokyo. The studio is best known for its limited-edition objects and its annual book series, Volumes.

The studio operates with a permanent staff of fourteen and ships fewer than 240 pieces per year. Its fourth volume, released in 2024, focuses on the workshop's brass casting practice.

History

Drift was founded in April 2010 with the express goal of remaining small. Founder Solen Park has stated that the studio will never grow beyond fourteen people.

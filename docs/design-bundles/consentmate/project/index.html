07 — FAQ

Straight answers to the usual questions.

Is this legal or planning advice?
No. ConsentMate is a clerical drafting service. We prepare documents from information you supply. We are not lawyers, licensed planners, or LBPs. You are the applicant and responsible for reviewing everything before submission.
Will council actually accept this?
Councils accept applications from the applicant — that's you. They don't care whether a firm, a planner, or ConsentMate prepared the paperwork, provided it meets their required content. Every pack we deliver matches the council's published form requirements.
Why is this so much cheaper than a planner?
Traditional consultancies bill hourly and price jobs for complex, notified consents. A $1,500 sleepout consent isn't commercial for them. We use AI to do the drafting and a human QA pass to catch errors — one flat fee, one pipeline, no hourly.
What types of consent do you cover?
Non-notified residential resource consents: sleepouts, garages, decks, minor dwellings, fence heights, recession plane breaches, minor earthworks. We don't do notified consents, subdivision, or building consents at launch.
How long does it take?
Two to three business days from a completed intake form to a reviewed draft in your inbox. Council then has 20 working days to process a non-notified application.
What if council asks for more information (s92)?
If council issues a section 92 request, we'll help you respond for a small additional fee. Done-for-you customers get s92 responses included.
What about council fees?
Council fees are separate and paid direct to council. For a non-notified residential application, expect a deposit of around $2,000–$4,000 depending on complexity. Our fee covers drafting only.
What happens to my data?
Your intake and supporting documents are stored in NZ-region Google Workspace storage. We don't resell it, we don't train AI models on it, and we delete on request.

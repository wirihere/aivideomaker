# Design System — Singularity Convergence

## Overview

Singularity Convergence is a sacred-cosmic digital experience — an AI-assisted Bible study tool ("The Oracle") with the visual personality of a deep-space observatory crossed with a candlelit chapel. The page is built almost entirely from typography on a near-black canvas, with one luminous focal point per section (the gold atom-orbit emblem in the hero, gold CTA buttons elsewhere). Layout is centred, low-density, and vertically generous — every section gives the eye somewhere to rest. The motion language is implied rather than animated: a starfield shimmer, a glowing emblem, weighted serif text — calm, deliberate, ceremonial.

## Colors

- **Primary Surface**: `#0A0A0F` — cosmic black for the page background ("the void with stars")
- **Deepest**: `#050508` — used for layered depth, modal backgrounds
- **Primary Accent (Gold)**: `#C9A84C` — luminous brass; brand identity, primary CTAs, the atom emblem's halo
- **Accent Light (Gold-Light)**: `#E8D48B` — highlight hits and hover states; reads as candle-flame
- **Accent Dim (Gold-Dim)**: `rgba(201, 168, 76, 0.15)` — subtle gold glow underlays
- **Primary Text (Parchment)**: `#E0DDD5` — body copy and headings; warm cream against the void
- **Secondary Text (Muted)**: `#A89F8F` — descriptive text, captions, navigation

The palette has only ~3 working hues (black, gold, cream). Restraint is part of the brand.

## Typography

- **Serif (everything)**: **Georgia** at weights 400 + 700. Carries the entire visual identity — scriptural weight, classical authority, no modernist hedging. Wide letter-spacing on display text (`SINGULARITY`, `CONVERGENCE` are tracked ~0.1-0.2em).
- **Fallback Sans**: Arial 400 (probably for one or two utility labels).
- **Hierarchy on the hero**: huge serif H1 ("SINGULARITY"), smaller all-caps tracked sub ("CONVERGENCE"), italic tagline at body size, paragraphs in regular weight at body size.
- **Italics carry emotion** — the tagline "*The Bible has the answers. We just removed the agenda.*" is in italic Georgia and is the brand's emotional core.

## Elevation

Depth is light-based, not shadow-based. The atom emblem in the hero has a soft golden bloom radiating into the dark — that's the elevation pattern. Everything else sits on the black canvas without raised cards or floating layers. CTA buttons are flat with thin gold borders + filled gold for primary. Text gets a barely-perceptible glow when sized large (the stars in the background contribute ambient luminance). No drop shadows. No glass / blur. Nothing skeuomorphic.

## Components

- **Atom Emblem**: gold-stroked orbits around a glowing core, surrounded by a soft halo. The single most identity-loaded asset on the site. Reads as both "atom" (science) and "halo" (sacred). Should appear in EVERY scene of any video — varied (zoomed, off-centre, dimmed, tilted) but present.
- **Starfield Background**: tiny gold pixels scattered on the void. Procedurally generated (probably). Calm, not aggressive.
- **Tracked Display Text**: massive Georgia caps with wide spacing, gold or parchment colored. The visual workhorse.
- **Italic Tagline Bar**: italic Georgia at moderate size, parchment colored. Carries emotional weight.
- **Gold CTA Pair**: filled-gold primary ("Ask The Oracle") + outlined secondary ("Join Free"). Always paired.
- **Quiet Footer Nav**: lowercase, tracked, muted-gold link list at the bottom of the hero. Never the focus.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do's
- Lean into restraint — the brand is built on what it leaves out. Single focal point per scene.
- Use Georgia in serif italic for emotional moments, and serif regular caps for declarative ones.
- Let the atom emblem be the unifying visual through the whole video. Vary scale and position; never duplicate, never lose.
- Treat gold as light, not as a colour. Gold should glow, fade in, breathe — not sit static.
- Stretch scene durations — this is a brand that rewards 5+ second holds. Don't cut on every beat.
- Use real-world stock that matches the *contemplative* register: candle in dark, hands turning a page, light through stained glass, single figure looking up. Always natural light, low-key.

### Don'ts
- Do not use any colour outside the black-gold-cream palette. Any tint or hue shift breaks the spell.
- Do not use sans-serif headlines. Georgia is the brand. (Arial may appear in tiny utility labels.)
- Do not use bright solid backgrounds — stay in the void.
- Do not use kinetic-pop / synthetic effects (glitch bursts, RGB shifts, neon). They fight the sacred register completely.
- Do not use cliché "AI/tech" stock (futuristic dashboards, robot hands, neural-network animations) — those undermine the brand's "the AI is just an instrument; the wisdom is ancient" stance.
- Do not use happy-actor stock photography. No smiling models. No staged "people meeting in a coffee shop" energy. The mood is solitude + reverence.
- Do not centre everything mechanically — vary the typographic composition (centred for declarative moments, left-aligned + low for intimate ones).

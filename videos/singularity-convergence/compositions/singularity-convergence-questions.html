Singularity Convergence

What weighs on you
tonight?

What can you
not forgive?

What do you wish
you'd asked your father —
before he was gone?

What's the question
you've never said
out loud?

There's a parable
for every one.

An ancient story
written before
you were born.

That somehow
knows your name.

Singularity
Convergence.

An oracle that reads
scripture without
an agenda.

Ask.
The Oracle finds
the parable.

Ask
anything.

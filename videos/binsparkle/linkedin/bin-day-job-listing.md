## Description

On collection day, we put our customers' bins out and bring them back in. We're after locals in Hamilton to do exactly that — a simple weekly run with two short visits, no scrubbing and no gear.

**The job**
- Collection day, before the truck: wheel the customer's bins to the kerb.
- After the truck's been through: bring them back to their spot, lids down.
- Snap two quick photos (bins out, bins back) so the customer can see it's done.
- Every week — there's a collection every week in Hamilton, 52 a year.

**What you keep**
- **75% of every Bin Day subscription** ($60/month per property). The homes on your street are your income.
- **$0 to join** — no franchise fee, no sign-up cost, no equipment to buy.
- **Paid weekly**, straight to your bank via Stripe.

**How it works**
You're self-employed from day one — your hours, your suburbs, your business. We bring the customers and handle the payments; you do the runs. It's light outdoor work that fits around another job, study or family.

**You'd be a good fit if**
You live locally, you're reliable on collection mornings, and you like the idea of steady weekly income without a boss.

**Where**
Hamilton now — we're already cleaning bins here. Outside Hamilton? Apply anyway and we'll email the moment Bin Day opens in your area (we go suburb by suburb as locals sign up).

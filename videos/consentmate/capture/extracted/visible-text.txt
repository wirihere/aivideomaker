[h2] Straight answers
[em] to the usual questions.
[summary] Is this legal or planning advice?
[div] No. We're a drafting service. We prepare documents from info you give us. We're not lawyers, licensed planners or licensed builders. You're the applicant — you review and sign everything before it goes to council.
[summary] Will council actually accept this?
[div] Yes. We draft the same documents a planner would: the Environmental Effects Report, cover letter, rule check, and the council's own application form. Council processes it like any other application — it doesn't matter who prepared it, only that it's complete.
[summary] Why is this so much cheaper than a planner?
[div] Planners bill by the hour, plus meetings, plus scope fees. A small residential consent isn't worth their time at a fixed price. We use AI to do the typing and a real person to check every page — which cuts 15–20 hours of manual work. That's where the savings come from.
[summary] How is this different from a LIM report?
[div] A LIM ($200–$300, ~2 weeks) lists your site's history — old consents, drain lines, hazards — and can include stuff we can't see (covenants, easements). Useful, but it won't tell you if your build fits. Our Property Report (free, 40 seconds) checks your actual build against every rule that applies. Different jobs.
[summary] What kinds of consent do you cover?
[div] Small residential resource consents — the kind you don't need neighbour approval for. Sleepouts, decks, carports, minor additions, garages, small boundary-rule breaches. If you're not sure whether yours fits, do the free property check first.
[summary] How long does it take?
[div] The property check itself is about 40 seconds. Once you send your ~8 answers, the drafted pack lands in your inbox in 2–3 business days. Council then has 20 working days to decide.
[summary] What if council asks for more info?
[div] If council writes back asking for more info (they call it "s92"), we help you answer — small extra fee. The attachment checklist we send you is designed to head most of these off.
[summary] What about the council fee?
[div] Council charges a separate fee — usually $500–$1,500 for small residential work, paid straight to them. Our fee is just for the drafting. Your Property Report shows your council's estimate.
[summary] What happens to my information?
[div] Your answers and documents sit in your own Google Drive folder. We don't resell them, we don't train AI on them, and we'll delete them on request after you lodge.

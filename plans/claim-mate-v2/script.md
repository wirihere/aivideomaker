# Claim Mate v2 — Script

**Brief:** 25–30s cinematic vertical promo for New Zealand ACC claimants who received a decline letter
**Length:** 27s target (range 25–30s)
**Format:** 1080×1920 vertical (9:16)
**Vibe:** Documentary calm, quiet authority
**Voice:** en-NZ-MollyNeural @ -10% rate

---

## Story arc

Structure: Problem → Reframe → Solution → Cost → CTA

The central tension is the moment a letter arrives saying no — and the promise is that a decline is not a dead end, it is the start of a process that is mostly handled for you, at no cost. The video does not argue or hype; it narrates a procedure with the calm confidence of someone who has done it before and will do it again.

---

## Beats

### Beat 1 — Hook: the letter, the no
- **Time:** ~0.0–4.0s
- **Narration:** "ACC said no."
- **Visual direction:** Two shots. First: a printed letter, face down on a kitchen table — just the corner showing, edge light. Cold, still. Second: the word DECLINED stamped across the page, revealed slowly. The mood is recognition, not melodrama — the viewer has seen this letter.
- **On-screen text:** None.
- **Notes:** Three words, long pause before the next beat. Molly's delivery should land in silence. The cut between shot 1 and shot 2 happens on the pause after "no."

---

### Beat 2 — Reframe: not the end
- **Time:** ~4.0–7.5s
- **Narration:** "A decline isn't the end."
- **Visual direction:** Two shots. First: a hand setting the letter aside — deliberate, unhurried. Second: the same desk, wider, with a laptop open and a cup of coffee — someone returning to ordinary life, not defeated. Mood shifts: warmer, forward-looking without being cheerful.
- **On-screen text:** None.
- **Notes:** Pause before and after this line. The mood pivot happens on the cut, not the words. Let the warmth in the second shot do the reframing work.

---

### Beat 3 — Process: step 1 (upload)
- **Time:** ~7.5–11.5s
- **Narration:** "Upload your letter. Two minutes."
- **Visual direction:** Single shot that carries two micro-moments. A person photographing a document with their phone — the act is simple, quick. The frame holds a beat after the photo is taken: done. Clean, functional aesthetic, top-down or slight angle. No clutter.
- **On-screen text:** STEP 01 · UPLOAD LETTER
- **Notes:** The brevity of the line matches the brevity of the action. No padding.
- **Source citation:** "Takes 2 minutes. Name, claim number, what ACC said no to." — Step 01, How it works section.

---

### Beat 4 — Process: step 2 (case check)
- **Time:** ~11.5–14.5s
- **Narration:** "We check your case."
- **Visual direction:** Two shots. First: text-heavy screen or document visible at a desk — the reviewing work, implied. Second: a brief glimpse of a tick or confirmation. Functional and assured, not dramatic. The "we" in this line carries weight — the viewer is not alone.
- **On-screen text:** STEP 02 · WE CHECK THE CASE
- **Notes:** Short line, breathing room after it. The pause between Beat 4 and Beat 5 is where the editor can hold on the confirmation shot.
- **Source citation:** "We check the case is reviewable." — Step 02, How it works section.

---

### Beat 5 — Process: step 3 (draft and lodge)
- **Time:** ~14.5–17.5s
- **Narration:** "We draft your review and lodge it within five days."
- **Visual direction:** Two shots. First: someone typing at a desk — purposeful, not hurried. Second: a progress indicator or document sent state — lodged. Documentary-still quality. This beat carries the "mostly us" promise.
- **On-screen text:** STEP 03 · DRAFT + LODGE
- **Notes:** "within five days" is the key fact. Molly should land the number clearly without rush.
- **Source citation:** "We draft your review and lodge it with FairWay within 5 days." — hero lede. "Five business days from go-ahead to lodgement with FairWay." — Step 03, How it works section.
- **Flag:** "within five days" is a paraphrase condensing "5 days" from hero meta and "Five business days" from step 03. The number is from the canonical source. The word "business" is dropped to keep the line under 12 words — this is a deliberate compression, not an invented claim.

### Beat 6 — Process: step 4 visual-only (FairWay hearing)
- **Time:** ~17.5–19.5s (visual interlude, no narration)
- **Narration:** [silence]

- **On-screen text:** STEP 04 · FAIRWAY HEARING · ABOUT 4 WEEKS
- **Notes:** ESC-01 — no narration for step 4. The 2-second hold gives the viewer time to read it. Music bed carries the silence.

### Beat 7 — Cost: you pay nothing
- **Time:** ~19.5–23.0s
- **Narration:** "You pay nothing. ACC pays our fee."
- **Visual direction:** Two shots. First: a clean typographic frame — "YOU PAY" on one side, "$0" on the other, ledger-style. Second: pull back slightly to show the full frame — stark and settled. The $0 figure is the visual anchor; the narration confirms it. No busy detail, no small print visible.
- **On-screen text:** YOU PAY · $0.00
- **Notes:** This line is a near-direct quote. The visual should carry the same weight as a signed document. Pull-back camera move on the $0 card gives it presence.
- **Source citation:** "You pay $0 — ACC pays our fee." — meta description and tape/header. "You pay nothing. ACC pays our fee when your review succeeds — or when your case had merit." — hero lede.
- **Flag:** "You pay nothing. ACC pays our fee." condenses the hero lede by dropping the conditional clause ("when your review succeeds — or when your case had merit"). The $0 claim is unconditional and stated in the meta description. The conditional nuance is preserved in the landing page for viewers who follow the URL.

### Beat 8 — CTA: start today
- **Time:** ~23.0–27.0s

- **On-screen text:** CLAIM/MATE · claim-mate.co.nz · Start your free review · Fine print below: "3 months from your decision letter"
- **Notes:** ESC-04 — "3 months from your decision letter" appears as on-screen fine print below the CTA. It is not narrated. The wordmark renders exactly as the site: uppercase, JetBrains Mono 700, navy slash.
- **Source citation:** "Start your free review" — CTA section. "claim-mate.co.nz" — canonical URL. "3 months from your decision letter" — hero meta, FAQ ("Three months from the date on your ACC decision letter").
